## 🎙️ Nvidia's Leadership Reset and the Future of Capital Backstops

After 33 years, Jensen Huang resigned from Nvidia. For now, Nvidia's board has appointed CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO and former Synopsys CEO Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever board chair. A formal CEO search reportedly considered Arm CEO Rene Haas.

* [Jensen Huang resigns](https://www.techmeme.com/261112/p26)
* [Nvidia appoints Harvey C. Jones board chair](https://www.techmeme.com/261112/p29)
* [Nvidia considers Arm CEO Rene Haas for now open CEO role](https://www.techmeme.com/261113/p17)

Silicon roadmaps aside, the bigger balance-sheet engineering questions are around how Nvidia deployed $48B across over 200 private companies and extended $165B in cloud backstops to invigorate the ecosystem demand for AI. Previously, Nvidia planned $500B in lending but the speculation is that the interim CEO (CFO) approach might be more measured than the Jensen Huang era.

* [Nvidia's $48B commitment question](https://www.techmeme.com/261113/p24)
* [Nvidia's $500B capital deployment question](https://www.techmeme.com/261113/p20)
* [Interim CEO approach speculation begins](https://www.techmeme.com/261112/p43)

## 📍 Field Report: Infrastructure Debt Discipline and Network Resilience

Across the data center stack, fiscal discipline is tightening. Debt restructures, physical power commitments, and the cost of capital is featuring more prominently in news coverage. Remember, there's the adage about the difference in debt and debut being "u" if you buy into the IPO frenzy. If an IPO is a means to seek additional funding to fulfill AI spend commitments, it is worth asking why "u" are paying that bill.

* [Nscale reaches $5.5B debt before IPO debut](https://www.techmeme.com/261113/p11)
* [Nscale funding gap for 2027 commitments](https://www.techmeme.com/261113/p3)
* [CoreWeave seeks $4B+ loan](https://www.techmeme.com/261111/p47)
* [SoftBank eats $3.7B in canceled power orders](https://www.techmeme.com/261111/p18)

DNS haiku memes aside, this week was a stark reminder the network matters. Allegedly, routine network updates triggered multi-day disruptions and increased visibility for the importance of rock solid dependeable resilience in all things network related. So, for all of the ink spilled about hyperscale compute clusters scaling into the gigawatts of power consumption, it's the boring old network change management that still remains the most critical thing to get right for making infrastructure (more) invisible.

* [Microsoft 365 outage stretches into second day](https://www.techmeme.com/261113/p21)
