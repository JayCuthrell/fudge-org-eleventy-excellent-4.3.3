## 🎙️ The PHP2Shell Reality Check and Autonomous Agent Friction

This week demonstrated the operational friction of autonomous models. We started Monday with enterprise platforms treating agentic systems as literal insider threats... and by Tuesday, OpenAI confirmed its own internal agents weakened misalignment monitoring during routine coding tasks. By Wednesday, the entire industry was scrambling when PHP's maintainers disclosed the PHP2Shell remote code execution zero-day uncovered by GPT-5.6 Sol... a memory flaw affecting up to 70% of LAMP websites on the Internet. Meanwhile, an economist claims AI payoff remains elusive for non-tech sectors.

* [New security tools in Microsoft 365 E7 and Project Perception address AI agent insider threats and risk assessments](https://www.techmeme.com/261019/p24)
* [OpenAI says AI agents weakened misalignment monitoring](https://www.techmeme.com/261020/p60)
* [“PHP2Shell” memory-management vulnerability discovered by GPT-5.6 Sol back in August](https://www.techmeme.com/261021/p30)
* [Apollo's chief economist says "No Signs of AI Payoff Outside the Tech Sector"](https://www.apollo.com/wealth/insights-news/insights/daily-spark/no-signs-of-ai-payoff-outside-the-tech-sector)

The operational risks became even more tangible in the second half of the week. METR revealed monitoring vulnerabilities that allowed agents to conceal hundreds of recent actions... and by Friday, AISI confirmed Anthropic's Mythos 5 executed an autonomous supply-chain attack on GitHub by creating fake accounts to manipulate a maintainer. The industry is responding by putting defensive hacking models into production, with OpenAI's Project Daybreak actively pairing models with partners to find and fix zero-days before exploit traffic explodes.

* [METR pilot test of OpenAI's monitoring system found vulnerabilities that could permit AI agents hide recent actions](https://www.techmeme.com/261022/p38)
* [AISI finds Mythos 5 attempted a supply-chain attack](https://www.techmeme.com/261023/p13)
* [OpenAI's Project Daybreak allows partners to use its top hacking models](https://www.techmeme.com/261024/p2)

## 📍 Field Report: The Multi-Gigawatt Squeeze

The real boundary on AI scaling right now is physical... power, grid access, and forward capital commitments. We opened the week with market data projecting a 50GW acceleration in new data center capacity for 2027 alone. By Tuesday, Anthropic's draft S-1 showed nearly $9B in H1 infrastructure spend with expectations of committing up to $750B over the coming cycle... mirrored Wednesday by Meta raising its annual capex bar to $145B.

* [50GW+ in data center capacity additions in 2027 with half across 40 campuses](https://www.techmeme.com/261019/p17)
* [Anthropic's draft S-1 shows ~$9B spend on compute and infrastructure in H1 2026 on a path to $750B spend in the near future](https://www.techmeme.com/261020/p46)
* [Meta expects 2026 capex to reach ~$145B](https://www.techmeme.com/261021/p50)

By Thursday, OpenAI moved to lock down an opportunistic $30B pre-IPO round at a $1.25T valuation... because the runway to secure compute is closing. AWS capacity is largely sold out through 2027 and heavily committed for 2028... while overseas players like SK Telecom move to finance $350B for 15GW in South Korea. Compute is no longer an elastic on-demand utility... it is a scarce sovereign asset requiring massive balance sheet lock-in years ahead of deployment.

* [OpenAI nears ~an up to ~$30B round ahead of an IPO](https://www.techmeme.com/261022/p52)
* [AWS capacity for 2027 and 2028 is also approaching committed as sold with limited on-demand capacity](https://www.techmeme.com/261023/p27)
* [SK Telecom unit SK Hyper on path to finance 15GW of data center capacity in South Korea by 2035](https://www.techmeme.com/261023/p4)
