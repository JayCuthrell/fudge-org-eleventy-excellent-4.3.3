## 📍 Field Report: Silicon Licensing, Off-Balance-Sheet Compute, and Power Scale

At the physical layer, US licensing rules are shaping how compute capacity gets allocated. Nscale will be examined according to those US licensing rules before being allowed to provide remote access to Nvidia GPUs. Considering ByteDance is almost 3/4 of Nscale's revenue last year, this will be a precedent setting examination for other neoclouds that follow. At the same time, Intel and SK Hynix are making growth and protective moves while Nvidia faces current challenges on multiple fronts.

* [ByteDance GPU access via Nscale requires regulatory approval](https://www.techmeme.com/261120/p3)
* [Intel signs first 18A-P customer](https://www.techmeme.com/261118/p59)
* [SK Hynix is now Kioxia's biggest shareholder](https://www.techmeme.com/261120/p8)

Financing massive power infrastructure requires operators to venture into greater financial engineering territory. The International Energy Agency anticipates 100 major projects requiring 50GW of power by 2030 that will need trillions in total funding. That kind of captial requirement means operators are turning to special lease structures.

* [$4T in data center spend by 2030](https://www.techmeme.com/261119/p24)
* [Apollo $40B GPU lease](https://www.techmeme.com/261118/p24)
* [Oracle funding OpenAI compute buildout](https://www.techmeme.com/261120/p43)
