## 🎙️ Regulatory Reset

The political spectrum for technology policy may have changed week with the US midterm elections. The prior mandate around the underlying infrastructure layer of the AI boom might change too.

All companies in the AI value chain tied to data centers may see renewed focused on regulation in the US market. Building out future AI data center capacity in the US might require more than exhuberance and assumed entitlements.

Unconstrained AI data center overbuilding will see headwinds as voters in dozens of US jurisdictions passed bans or restrictions. This represents a shift for prioritizing AI regulation and AI value chain scrutiny that will mark the end of the recent "build first, ask questions later" expansion thesis.

## 📍 Field Report: Silicon to Services Shuffle

Beneath the elections outcomes, the AI hardware value chain is experiencing realignment from the silicon to services layers. Unusual partnerships, strategic shifts, and tactical decisions will seek to address econimic expectations while addressing ongoing market demand with anticipated regulatory headwinds.

Expect to see GPUs alone becomes less important than to managing infrastructure lifecycle more effectively and efficiently. Also, expect capital expenditures, operating efficiencies, and securing essential vertical integration to be increasingly identifiable patterns. 

As the physical limits of AI hardware scaling arrive in manufacturing and deployment, the algorithmic differences in succesful and unsuccessful companies will be operational excellence within shifting market conditions. So, it is likely that services will become more differentiated in terms of unit cost transparency across operations, security, and resilience rather than just an ability for standing up current silicon at scale.
