## 📍 Field Report: Quantum Capital and Validation at Scale

* **VC Quantum Interest Rising:** VCs reached +$4B in quantum computing companies investments for 2026. More: [FT: Can quantum turn ‘magic’ into money?](https://www.ft.com/content/ae9eedd2-4530-47e4-be4b-242d0e2a6253) and [meta coverage](https://www.techmeme.com/260926/p7#a260926p7)
* **Quantum Topological Test:** Microsoft's Majorana 2 chip quantum research center is now open to DARPA evaluation teams for testing the topological architecture. More: [Reuters: Microsoft to give DARPA hands-on access to quantum system at new Maryland site](https://www.reuters.com/business/microsoft-give-darpa-hands-on-access-to-quantum-system-new-maryland-site-2026-09-22/) and [meta coverage](https://www.techmeme.com/260922/p54#a260922p54)

## 🚀 Infrastructure: Orbital AI Compute and Terrestrial Enterprise Agentic OS

* **Orbital AI:** Google's "Project Suncatcher" will launch on October 1, 2026 with four custom Trillium TPUs that operate on solar. More: [NYT: Google Takes the A.I. Data Center Race to Outer Space](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/24/technology/google-suncatcher-ai-data-center-space.html) and [meta coverage](https://www.techmeme.com/260924/p28#a260924p28)
* **Enterprise Agentic OS:** Microsoft Copilot now consolidates enterprise tools into a unified AI harness. More: [The Verge: Microsoft thinks its new Copilot ‘super app’ will be as influential as Office](https://www.theverge.com/news/1000532/microsoft-copilot-super-app-chat-coding-autopilot) and [meta coverage](https://www.techmeme.com/260925/p5#a260925p5)

## 🧬 Software: Autonomous Science and Biosurveillance in a Box

* **Autonomous Scientific Discovery:** It appears that a swarm of AI agents have autonomously discovered an enzyme system hidden within the DNA of bacteriophages that is similar to CRISPR arrays. More: [The Verge: Anthropic’s biolab made a discovery it’s comparing to Crispr](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/999470/anthropic-biolab-claude-crispr) and [meta coverage](https://www.techmeme.com/260923/p34#a260923p34)
* **Biosurveillance:** Pilgrim took $25M seed to develop a device that combines air sampling and localized genomic sequencing for detecting and identifying airborne biological threats. More: [WSJ: Anthropic Leaders Back Startup Developing System to Detect Biological Threats](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/anthropic-leaders-back-startup-developing-system-to-detect-biological-threats-88ef9f0a) and [meta coverage](https://www.techmeme.com/260923/p33#a260923p33)
