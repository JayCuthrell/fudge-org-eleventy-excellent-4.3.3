    badge or favicon after running git checkout.

    (Your Mileage May Vary).

#mastodonadmin note on v4.1.3 is to remember the dist/nginx.conf added two lines to location \~^/system/

add\_header X-Content-Type-Options nosniff;  
add\_header Content-Security-Policy "default-src 'none'; form-action 'none'";

And... you'll need to redo and badge or favicon changes you made after you git checkout v4.1.3

YMMV
