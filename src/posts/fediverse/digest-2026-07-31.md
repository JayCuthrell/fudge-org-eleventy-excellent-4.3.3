### 10:35 PM

{% raw %}
Started down the path to a [#selfhosted](https://cuthrell.com/tags/selfhosted) [#portainer](https://cuthrell.com/tags/portainer) [#docker](https://cuthrell.com/tags/docker), newsletter engine digester after finding an old Raspberry Pi 4 with 8GB RAM that I forgot about and the power adapter to my old Dell laptop with 4GB RAM.

  * Daily: Laptop runs one [#n8n](https://cuthrell.com/tags/n8n) workflow that fetches emails, sanitizes text via JS, and tasks a Pi running [#ollama](https://cuthrell.com/tags/ollama) (qwen2.5:1.5b) with a 3-bullet summary, then logs to local [#postgres](https://cuthrell.com/tags/postgres).
  * Weekly: Laptop runs another similar n8n workflow late Saturday night to aggregates the logs, extracts macro-trends via Pi, and email me a brief.



This is both awful and wonderful but with zero cloud fees, completely local, and doesn't require sub-millisecond response times.

Next: Find slightly more suitable hardware after I get this tuned.
{% endraw %}
