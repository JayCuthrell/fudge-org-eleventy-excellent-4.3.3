**[fudge.org](/)** is the digital hub for **Fudge Factor** and the **What The Fudge** podcast by [Jay Cuthrell](https://jaycuthrell.com). Built with Eleventy and focused on POSSE (Publish on Own Site, Syndicate Everywhere), the site covers technology market shifts and convergence.

---

## About Jay Cuthrell

I am a transformational technology executive, operator, and board advisor based in Beaufort, North Carolina. Currently serving as Chief Product Officer at [NexusTek](https://nexustek.com), I operate at the intersection of product strategy, managed intelligence, and organizational scale. Over the last 25+ years, my career has spanned venture-backed startups, private equity turnarounds, and global Fortune 50 environments.
